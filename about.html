Who

I'm an incident responder in a managed SOC, six years in. I started in alert triage and now handle incident response escalations for roughly 90 client environments: ransomware, initial-access compromises, business email compromise, and insider cases, through containment, eradication, and the post-incident report.

What I do

  • Incident response: scoping, containment, eradication, and reporting for clients, legal, and cyber insurance
  • Malware analysis: static reverse engineering of loaders and RATs with Ghidra, Binary Ninja, capa, FLOSS, and x64dbg
  • Detection engineering: KQL for Defender XDR and Sentinel, plus indicator rules pushed to Defender
  • Forensics: KAPE, Velociraptor, Eric Zimmerman's tools, Volatility, MemProcFS, Plaso

Certifications and training

  • GIAC GCFA and GCIH
  • Microsoft SC-200 and AZ-104
  • Malware reverse engineering: TheMalwareLab (Anuj Soni)

How these writeups work

  • They come from real cases or lab analysis, with client, user, and device details removed and attacker infrastructure defanged
  • Each has a TL;DR, an attack chain, and a How I know section that labels every claim with its evidence and my confidence, so verified and inferred stay separate
  • Techniques are mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, with IOC tables and hunting queries where I have real evidence to back them

The Toolkit and Artifacts pages are the command and artifact references I keep open while working.
